What’s in the ground here

Most of Delaware County sits on Wissahickon schist and gneiss, the same hard, layered rock you see along the Crum and Ridley creeks. Along the Delaware River, from Tinicum down through Chester, the ground turns to Coastal Plain sand, gravel and clay.

Permits and local rules

The Delaware County Conservation District handles erosion and sediment control plans and NPDES permits. Floodplain rules matter here: Darby, Cobbs, Crum and Ridley creeks all have mapped flood zones, and work near them gets extra review.

Delaware County questions

My yard floods every storm. Can you help?

Usually. Many Delaware County drainage problems are fixed with regrading, a swale and better downspout routing. Near a creek, we check the floodplain rules before moving any dirt.

Do you work in eastern Delco?

Yes, including Upper Darby, Drexel Hill and Lansdowne, where tight lots and shared driveways call for compact equipment.
