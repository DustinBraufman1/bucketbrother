Land & Lot Clearing in Greater Philadelphia

Overgrown lots, fence lines swallowed by brush, stumps where a house needs to go. We clear to the ground you need, keep the trees you want and haul the rest away.

What’s included

  • Brush and small tree removal
  • Stump and root removal
  • Overgrown lot and fence-line cleanup
  • Invasive thickets, including multiflora rose and bamboo
  • Rock and debris removal
  • Storm damage cleanup

Check before you clear

Clearing is where projects most often run into rules people didn’t know about. Work in or near streams and wetlands can need a state Chapter 105 permit. Many townships in the region also protect stream buffers, steep slopes and larger trees with their own ordinances.

We check the local rules for your address before the first stump comes out.

What happens to the brush and stumps

Brush and wood are hauled away or chipped. Stumps come out with the root ball so the ground can be graded and built on. We fill and compact the holes, and if the site is staying open we can finish with topsoil ready for seed.

What affects the price

  • Acreage and density of growth
  • Size and number of stumps
  • Slope, wet ground and access
  • Hauling versus keeping material on site
  • Trees and features to protect
  • Final grading and topsoil

Every estimate is free, written and itemized, so you can see where the money goes.

Land Clearing questions

Do I need a permit to clear my land?

Small residential cleanups usually don’t, but larger clearing, work near streams or wetlands, and removing protected trees can. Rules vary by township, so we check the local ordinance before we start.

Can you leave some of the trees?

Yes. Walk the lot with us and flag what stays. We work around those trees and keep heavy equipment off their root zones as much as the site allows.

Will you remove the stumps too?

Yes. Clearing for building or grading means pulling stumps and roots, filling the holes and compacting so the ground doesn’t settle later.
