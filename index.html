FAQ

Excavation questions, answered

Don’t see yours? Call (215) 555-0100.

How much does excavation cost in the Philadelphia area?

It depends on the size and depth of the job, access, what’s in the ground and how much soil has to be hauled away. We don’t post flat prices because two jobs that sound the same rarely are. We walk the site and give you a written, itemized estimate at no charge.

Do I need a permit for excavation or grading?

Often. Pennsylvania requires a written erosion and sediment control plan once a project disturbs 5,000 square feet, and an NPDES permit at one acre. Townships and the City of Philadelphia add their own permit rules. We help you figure out what applies before work is scheduled.

Do you call PA811 before digging?

Every time. State law requires a PA One Call request at least three business days before any excavation. We place the request and wait for the marks before we dig.

Do you work for homeowners or just builders?

Both. We work for homeowners, builders, developers, property managers and businesses, from a single drainage fix to full site work for new construction.

What areas do you serve?

Philadelphia and Bucks, Montgomery, Delaware and Chester Counties in southeastern Pennsylvania.

Are you registered and insured?

Yes. Our Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration number is PA000000, and we can send a certificate of insurance on request.
